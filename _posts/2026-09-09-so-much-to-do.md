---
title: So Much to Do Before I Kill Myself
---

My assignments. I haven't turned them in yet.

I think my professors would be slightly irritated if I didn't.

My students. The semester isn't over yet. I'm not sure if they care.

But I think it'd be inconvenient for everyone involved if I stopped.

My friends. My family. My superiors.

I make them happy. I think. I'm not sure what they find in me.

But I guess they wouldn't really be happy if I stopped doing that.

My projects. My dreams. My future.

I would be somewhat annoyed if I didn't finish what I started.

My husband. I'm not sure who he is yet.

But he'd be kind of pissed if he never got to meet me.

I'd be pissed too, probably.

So perhaps I can hold on for just a little bit longer.
